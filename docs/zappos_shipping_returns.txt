ZAPPOS SHIPPING POLICY — Paraphrased Summary

Zappos offers free standard shipping on all orders with no minimum purchase required.
Standard orders are typically delivered within 4 to 5 business days. Zappos also
provides expedited shipping options at checkout, including next-day delivery for
eligible items when ordered before the cut-off time shown on the product page.

All orders are processed and dispatched from Zappos warehouse facilities. Once an
order ships, customers receive a confirmation email containing a tracking number.
Shipments can be tracked through the carrier's website or via the Zappos order
status page using the customer's account or order ID.

Zappos ships to all 50 US states, US territories, and APO/FPO military addresses.
International shipping is not offered directly through zappos.com. Orders shipped
to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories may require additional transit time beyond
the standard delivery estimate.

For items marked "Ships Separately," fulfilment may occur from different warehouse
locations, resulting in multiple tracking numbers for a single order. Customers are
notified of each shipment individually.

Zappos does not charge additional fees for signature-required deliveries. If a
delivery attempt is unsuccessful, the carrier will leave a notice and attempt
re-delivery on the next business day. Unclaimed packages are returned to Zappos
after three unsuccessful attempts; the customer receives a full refund upon return.

Large or bulky items may ship via freight carrier. Freight shipments include
curbside delivery and require a scheduled delivery appointment arranged by the
carrier directly with the customer.
