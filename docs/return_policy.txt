RETURN POLICY — Effective January 2024

1. ELIGIBILITY
   Items may be returned within 30 days of the original purchase date.
   All returned items must be in their original, unopened packaging.
   Software licences and digital downloads are non-refundable once activated.

2. PROCESS
   To initiate a return, contact support@example.com with your order ID and reason.
   Our team will issue a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number within 2 business days.
   Ship the item back using a tracked courier. We do not cover return shipping costs.

3. REFUNDS
   Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days of receiving the returned item.
   Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.
   A 10% restocking fee applies to all hardware returns.

4. EXCEPTIONS
   Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned.
   Damaged goods caused by misuse are not eligible for a refund.
   Services are non-refundable once rendered.
