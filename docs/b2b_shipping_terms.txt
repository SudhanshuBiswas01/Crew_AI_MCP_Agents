B2B SHIPPING TERMS — Standard Operations Guide

All outbound shipments are processed through the company's designated carrier
accounts. The primary carrier for ground domestic shipments is UPS; FedEx is used
as the secondary carrier for time-sensitive and air freight shipments. Carrier
selection is made automatically by the warehouse management system based on the
destination zone, weight class, and requested delivery speed.

Ground shipping transit times range from 1 to 5 business days depending on origin
and destination zones. Zone 2 shipments (within 150 miles of the fulfilment centre)
typically arrive in 1 to 2 business days. Zone 8 shipments (cross-country) take 4
to 5 business days under standard ground service.

All shipments over 5 kg or valued above $200 are automatically insured against loss
or damage in transit. Claims for lost or damaged shipments must be filed within 9
calendar days of the estimated delivery date. Claims submitted outside this window
will not be accepted by the carrier.

Hazardous materials, lithium batteries shipped separately, and oversized items
(longest dimension exceeding 108 inches or combined length plus girth exceeding 165
inches) require specialised handling and must be booked through the freight desk
at least 48 hours before the scheduled pick-up.

All shipping labels are generated by the fulfilment team and must include the
customer's order ID in the reference field to enable automatic delivery confirmation
matching in the order management system. Shipments missing a valid order ID in the
reference field are logged as exceptions and require manual resolution.
