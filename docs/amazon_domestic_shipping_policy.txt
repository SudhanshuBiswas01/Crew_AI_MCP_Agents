Amazon Domestic Shipping Policy — Internal Reference

This internal reference document details shipping guidelines, delivery speeds, and member benefits for domestic fulfillment. For standard shipping, non-Prime members receive their orders within four to five business days. For faster delivery, customers can purchase expedited shipping, which reduces transit times to two to four business days.

Non-Prime members receive free standard shipping only on orders totaling over thirty-five dollars. Amazon Prime members receive free two-day delivery on eligible items. Prime members also qualify for free one-day shipping on eligible products, and free same-day shipping in over nine thousand cities. The cutoff time for same-day delivery is displayed on the product detail page, with deliveries completed by ten PM local time. Prime membership is priced at one hundred and thirty-nine dollars annually or fourteen dollars and ninety-nine cents monthly.

Amazon utilizes a diverse logistics network. Carrier services include United Parcel Service (UPS), United States Postal Service (USPS), and Amazon Logistics. The carrier is assigned automatically at dispatch based on speed and region. 

To prevent theft, orders valued over one thousand three hundred dollars require a signature upon delivery. Operations staff must verify that signature service is active for high-value items during packaging. Customers receive tracking links for all shipments. Customer service agents can verify shipping status and carrier details through the internal administration panel.
