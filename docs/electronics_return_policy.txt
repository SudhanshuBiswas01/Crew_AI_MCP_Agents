ELECTRONICS RETAILER RETURN POLICY — Standard Terms

Most electronics products may be returned or exchanged within 15 days of the
original purchase date. Members enrolled in the premium loyalty tier have an
extended return window of 30 days on most products. Activatable devices, including
smartphones and cellular tablets, must be returned within 14 days regardless of
membership status.

To be eligible for a return, items must include all original contents: the product
itself, all accessories, the original packaging, and any included documentation.
Items returned without original packaging may be subject to a restocking fee of up
to 15 percent of the purchase price.

Televisions, major appliances, and open-box items are subject to specialised return
procedures. Televisions must be inspected for visible damage at the time of delivery
or in-store collection; damage reported after the initial inspection period may not
qualify for a no-cost return.

Software, movies, music, and video games that have been opened are not eligible for
a full refund unless the product is defective. In such cases, the customer may
exchange the item for the same title only.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method. In-store purchases may receive
a cash refund for cash transactions or a credit to the original card for card
transactions. Online returns processed by mail take up to 7 business days from
receipt to appear on the statement.

Geek Squad Protection plans and product subscriptions are non-refundable after the
initial cancellation window specified at time of purchase.
