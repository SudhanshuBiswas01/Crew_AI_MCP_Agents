# Source: synthetic — internal product operations note

Product Operations Note: USB-C 7-in-1 Hub
Product: USB-C 7-in-1 Hub
SKU: UCH-7001

This product note details the specifications, issues, and inventory for the USB-C Hub. The device features an HDMI port supporting 4K resolution at 60Hz, three USB-A 3.0 ports, a dual SD and microSD card reader, and a USB-C Power Delivery pass-through port supporting up to 100W of power.

Support reports indicate charging instability when input power exceeds 85W and the hub is connected to a MacBook M2 with Pro or Max chips. The workaround is to cap the Power Delivery input at 65W in the hub's settings, or plug a dedicated 140W charger directly into the laptop.

To resolve USB-A enumeration bugs found in firmware version 2.2.x, technicians recommend upgrading all inventory to firmware version 2.3.1.
