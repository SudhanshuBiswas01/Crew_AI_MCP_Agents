SUMMARY OF INCIDENT:

10 units of the Anker PowerExpand+ 7-in-1 USB-C Hub ($54.99 each, total

HDMI output on connected MacBook Pro M2 devices. Remaining 6 units function

"We deployed these across the team and four of them don't output to the external
monitor at all. We've tried different cables and monitors. The other six work
fine. This is blocking our remote work setup."
